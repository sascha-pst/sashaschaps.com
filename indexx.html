02 / About me

A marketer’s perspective.
A builder’s curiosity.

I’m Sasha, a senior lifecycle marketing manager interested in what people need—and how better experiences can meet them there.

I bring a marketing perspective to the questions I explore: who is this for, what would make it useful, and how will we know it’s working?

I’m expanding that practice through UC Berkeley’s online Master of Information and Data Science program, MIDS. I’m learning to code, work with data, and build products grounded in analysis and customer needs.

Start with people

Understand the need.

Ask who the work serves, where the friction is, and what a better experience would look like.

Follow the evidence

Make the question testable.

Look for patterns, check assumptions, and be clear about what the data can support.

Build to learn

Make something useful.

Turn the insight into a program, a story, or a prototype—and use what happens next to improve it.
