Background

Field notes

Seven years in content strategy, scaled enablement, and program development taught me how systems of people learn. UC Berkeley’s MIDS program is teaching me how systems of numbers do — with a focus on algorithmic fairness, and a soft spot for anything oceanic. The full story lives on the About page.

The pottery isn’t a metaphor. (Fine — it’s a little bit of a metaphor.) Wedging, layering, trimming: most of data work is preparing material honestly and knowing when to stop.
