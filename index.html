Nerikomi builds its pattern into the body of the clay, so a cross-section shows the same design as the surface. Analysis should hold up the same way — state the assumption, test it, name the confound that could undo it, then report what the data actually said. Every project below documents its own limitations.

An Observable notebook visualizing climate vulnerability across selected California fishery species. The underlying assessment is the work of a lab, state fisheries, and NOAA — my contribution is the analysis layer that makes it readable.

Bullish on Sharks

A global exploratory analysis of shark-human encounters from the Global Shark Attack File, asking how patterns shift across geography, activity, and time — and which factors associate with fatal outcomes. Reporting bias is the dominant confound, read at your own risk.
