About

Sasha Schaps — pattern all the way through

A Bay Area native finishing a master's in Data Science at UC Berkeley's School of Information, with a background in program management and content strategy. I'm drawn to problems where structure is doing quiet work underneath: ordered networks in nature, layered clay, the shape of a dataset before anyone has plotted it.

Away from the keyboard I work in nerikomi — colored clays laminated so the pattern runs through the body of the piece rather than sitting on its surface. It's a reasonable standard to hold analysis to: state the assumption, test it, name the confound, report what the data said.
