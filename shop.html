From clay
to kiln.

Every piece passes through my hands a dozen times over two to three weeks.

No.StepWhat happens
01WedgeClay is kneaded to push out air and even the moisture, so it won't crack in the kiln.Day 1
02ThrowCentered and pulled up on the wheel into its rough form.Day 1
03TrimAt leather-hard, the foot is carved and handles attached.Day 2–3
04BisqueA first, lower firing turns dry clay into porous ceramic that can take glaze.Week 2
05Glaze & fireDipped, wiped, and fired to about 2,200°F, where the glaze melts into glass.Week 3

Care &
questions.

Made for daily use, not a shelf.

Small variations in glaze, size and form are part of handmade, and the photos are representative of each line.

Is it food and dishwasher safe?

Yes. All glazes are lead-free and fired to stoneware temperature. Handwashing keeps them looking their best longest.

How long does shipping take?

Pieces ship within 3–5 business days, then usually take 2–6 days to arrive. Orders over $75 ship free.

What if something arrives broken?

Send a photo within 7 days and I'll replace it or refund you.

Do you take custom orders?

For sets of four or more, yes. Email me with what you have in mind.

Somethingcustom?

Dinnerware sets, gifts, a glaze you saw and want on a different form. Tell me about it.
